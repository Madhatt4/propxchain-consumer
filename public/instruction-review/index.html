What do you need to take this on?

The government's June roadmap points at sales packs prepared before a property is listed — searches included, gathered at the seller stage. We are building for that: the seller comes to us first, the searches are ordered and paid for up front, and the firm that takes the instruction receives a brief that is already complete.

We are not asking you to price anything off a screen. The question is narrower and it is yours to answer: what is the minimum you need before you would engage a client, and how would you want that introduction to reach you? Mark the lines below, answer two questions, press Send. Two minutes.
