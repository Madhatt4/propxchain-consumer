About PropXchain

PropXchain is an online platform for selling and buying a home in England and Wales. Sellers, buyers, conveyancers and estate agents work from the same live view of the transaction, and each milestone is recorded on the Internet Computer as a tamper-evident audit trail. Only cryptographic fingerprints of documents are written on-chain, never the documents themselves.

PropXchain is free to use for buyers and sellers, with no platform fee: people pay only for the third-party items they order, such as property searches and ID checks, each priced when they order. Conveyancers on the panel quote each transaction directly. PropXchain never holds client money.

PropXchain Ltd is registered in England and Wales (Companies House 17018978) and based in Sandy, Bedfordshire. It is not related to Propchain (PROPC), the crypto token.
