Why UK conveyancing is broken

  • 20–24 weeks to complete on average. Most of it spent waiting for solicitors to chase each other.
  • £1,500–£2,500 average seller cost, most of which is admin work rather than legal work.
  • 30%+ of transactions collapse before completion, largely because buyers and sellers have no visibility.
  • Zero real-time visibility:all information flows through solicitors who chase each other while you wait.

Frequently asked questions

How much does PropXchain cost?

Starting is free — you run your own transaction and see every provider's price before you commit, with no platform fee. The optional £75 Premium AI co-pilot reads your title and searches in plain English and tailors your conveyancer quote.

Is the blockchain audit trail legally valid in England and Wales?

Yes. Every action is recorded on the Internet Computer. Documents themselves stay on your device. Only their SHA-256 hashes are written to the blockchain, which keeps verification cryptographic but GDPR-compliant. Exchange of contracts is signed via tmSign QES for legal validity.

Does PropXchain connect to HM Land Registry?

Yes. PropXchain is integrated with HM Land Registry's Business Gateway, so title checks, official copies and registration of dealings happen inside the platform.

What about money laundering (AML) and identity (KYC) checks?

AML and KYC checks are handled in a single step via a regulated provider. The resulting AML certificate is stored on-chain and reusable with lenders and other regulated parties.

Is there a platform fee?

No. Buyers and sellers both use the Starter tier free, paying their own providers direct. The only optional charge is the £75 Premium AI co-pilot.

Which blockchain does PropXchain use?

The Internet Computer Protocol (ICP). PropXchain runs 12 canisters on ICP mainnet. There is no gas fee for users.

Does PropXchain replace my solicitor?

No. PropXchain is the coordination and verification platform. You still instruct a regulated conveyancer or solicitor; PropXchain simply removes the admin overhead so they can focus on legal work.
